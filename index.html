Sudha Pujari is a Product Security Engineer at Salesforce and an accomplished academic researcher with over 7 years of expertise in application security, cloud hardening, and secure software development lifecycle (SSDLC) architecture. She specializes in protecting critical systems across global enterprise platforms and bridging the gap between cutting-edge academic research and real-world engineering resilience.

As a public figure and thought leader, she is a Bronze Winner in the 2025 Globee® Awards for Cybersecurity, author of Cyber Hygiene for Digital Age (2025), published researcher across multiple high-impact journals, IEEE InC4 Session Chair, and a frequent lead judge for global innovation challenges including Jesse H. Neal Awards and Voxel51 AI Hackathons.

"Advancing cybersecurity through original research, secure-by-design innovation, and empowering the next generation of defenders."
